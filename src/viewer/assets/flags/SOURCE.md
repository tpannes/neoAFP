# Flags

Drawn for neoAFP from the official flag geometry and colors (national flags are not subject to
copyright). Simplified for display at about 20 × 14 pels: Spain without the coat of arms, the
United Kingdom cropped from 2:1 to 3:2.

Language → flag (see `language-menu.js`): en → gb, de → de, fr → fr, es → es, it → it,
nl → nl, zh-Hans → cn, ja → jp.
